What SOAR-Flow contributes

Protected interaction

CBF–MPC certifies policy references before they reach the controller and records the certified transition for learning.

Value calibration

Gaussian return variability smooths the Cal-QL calibration pressure and reduces policy degradation during online updates.

Adaptive guidance

Corrections teach feasible recovery while intervention-to-go prediction gradually reduces auxiliary guidance as dependence falls.
